Privacy policy: Umbrella mravki

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · mravki · Terms of service

What Umbrella mravki is

Umbrella mravki is a personal tool used only by the creator of the mravki video series (Filip Jordanoski, Skopje, North Macedonia). It publishes the series' own videos to the mravki accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, and reads those videos' statistics. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

Umbrella mravki uses YouTube API Services. Its use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, given when signing in with Google, Umbrella mravki:

  • uploads the series' videos to the mravki YouTube channel, with their titles, descriptions and tags;
  • reads the channel's and its videos' numbers: views, likes, comments, shares, watch time and subscribers.

It does not read or change anything else: no other channels, comments, messages, contacts, email or files.

How the data is kept

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. The videos' statistics are kept in the creator's private database, to show how the series is doing. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Umbrella mravki's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other platforms

The same applies to the other accounts: the tool signs in only to the mravki accounts on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and LinkedIn (through Upload-Post), only to publish the series' videos and read their numbers, and keeps their sign-in tokens on the creator's own computer.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Access for the other platforms can be removed in each platform's own settings.

Deleting data

Umbrella mravki keeps only the mravki accounts' own sign-in tokens and the statistics of their videos, on its creator's computer and private database. To have any data about you deleted, message mravki on Instagram or Facebook: it is deleted within 30 days. Removing the tool's access in Facebook (Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites) or on the Google security settings page stops it from reading anything at once.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message mravki on Instagram or Facebook.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
